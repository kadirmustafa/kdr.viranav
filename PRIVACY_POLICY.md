# Privacy Policy for ViraNav

**Last updated:** October 12, 2026

**ViraNav** ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

- **Location Data (GPS):**  
  ViraNav requests access to precise and approximate device location solely while the application is in active use. This data is utilized on-device to calculate live nautical speed, GPS heading, position coordinates, and offline navigational tracks.
- **Sensor Data:**  
  ViraNav accesses the device magnetometer and accelerometer to provide live compass heading and pitch/roll instrumentation.
- **Barometer / Atmospheric Data:**  
  If supported by your device, atmospheric pressure data is accessed to compute live barometric pressure and pressure trend alerts.

---

### 2. How We Use Information

- To display real-time navigation instrumentation, speed over ground (SOG), course over ground (COG), and nautical coordinates.
- To store offline GPS voyage logs locally on your device storage in an internal database.
- We **do not** transmit, sell, or share your personal location or route data to any third-party servers. All logbook entries remain strictly private on your device unless you explicitly export them.

---

### 3. Data Storage and Security

- Voyage data, bookmarks, and settings are saved locally on your device.
- No remote account registration or cloud sign-in is required to use ViraNav.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services

ViraNav may connect to public weather APIs (e.g. Open-Meteo) using coarse geographic coordinates solely to fetch current marine weather, wind, and swell conditions. No personally identifiable information (PII) is transmitted.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy

ViraNav is intended for general audiences and maritime navigation. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
